# Borea design direction

- Nord Polar Night surfaces and frost/ice blue highlights. No purple, violet, lavender or pink UI accents, including stock controls.
- Dark by default; the light appearance uses Nord Snow Storm and cool blue accents.
- Keep upstream logo artwork in its original colours. No geometric frames behind logos and no game-stat panels.
- Wind moves from right to left in irregular gusts of hard, straight streaks. Each eases in, accelerates with a motion blur that stretches with its speed, then drags to a crawl, fading in and out so it never visibly starts or stops. Page changes smoothly accelerate it, then it settles back to a calm pace.
- Selection has a crisp ice rim, a soft glow and one passing glint. Hover and keyboard focus remain distinct.
- Family cards wear each family's own primary colour (Arch #1793D1, Fedora #51A2DA, Debian #A80030, openSUSE #73BA25) and its own type where it publishes a free face. Hovering lifts a card with a deeper shadow and a family-coloured glow, and the side under the pointer gives way into shade. Picking one fades in a soft gradient of its colour that drifts slowly, with a 2 px rim and a gently breathing glow. No shimmer, glint or cursor glow on these cards.
- A family's card carries its own words (its project's published line or its founder's), credited, and its release type along the bottom. A family that isn't ready yet is greyed out, still, and says "Coming soon" at the bottom right.
- Pause stops all decorative motion. Static selection and focus remain clear. Avoid flashing effects and layout shifts.

These are the user's saved preferences and supersede the earlier purple Material palette.
