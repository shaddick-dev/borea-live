Bare, bold, keyboard first. Taiga dusk: spruce, lichen and birch, one warm cloudberry glow, and frost on the wind.

No boxes.
Type and space do the work. The only lines on screen are the wind and an underline under what you can change.
Big question, bold.
58px, weight 700, tight tracking, left aligned. Answers sit underneath at 22px; the chosen one goes bold with a frost arrow →.
Wind on screen.
Three faint hairlines drift right to left, 38–52 s each. Slow enough to notice, never enough to follow.
Wind between slides.
The old slide fades 16px left in 280 ms; the new one fades in from 20px right in 550 ms. One faint gust crosses, slowly. No blur, no bounce.
Progress is wind.
A row of ∼ fills as the install runs, and the wind picks up with it: more streaks, longer, faster, until one last gust at 100%. The number is always written too, so colour is never the only clue.
Stillness on request.
“motion off” in the footer, borea.motion=0 at boot, or the system's reduced-motion setting: wind lines vanish, slides switch instantly, nothing else changes.
